People clients can check in for

Each person gets a button on the kiosk. When a client taps it, that person gets an email.

    During the beta, each new email address needs a one-time confirmation before it receives notices. Contact us after adding someone new.

    Kiosks

    Open a kiosk link on your iPad in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, then lock it with Guided Access (Settings → Accessibility). Keep the link private: anyone with it can check clients in. Use Edit screens to choose what each kiosk shows and what its buttons do; changes reach the iPad within a few minutes.

      Email templates

      What the email says when a kiosk button notifies someone. Choose a template for each button in Edit screens; buttons without one send the Standard email. These tags are filled in when the email goes out: {Person} (who was notified), {Time}, {Kiosk}, {All answers} (everything the visitor entered), and any field name from your screens.

        Business details

        The phone number goes in the default “No appointment” screen. Alerts go to the alert email when someone can't be notified.

        Recent check-ins

        The last 25 taps on your kiosks. Nothing visitors type is recorded.
