Your lobby, checked in.
No front desk needed.

LobbyApp turns an iPad into a check-in kiosk. Clients tap the person they're here to see, and that person gets an email the moment they arrive.

How it works

1

Add your people

Create an account, then list everyone who sees clients, with the email address their check-in notices should go to.

2

Set up the iPad

Open your kiosk link in Safari, add it to the Home Screen and lock it with Guided Access. No app to install.

3

Clients check themselves in

They tap who they're here to see. That person gets an email right away, and the client is asked to take a seat.

Built for small offices

Private by design

The kiosk only asks what you set it up to ask. Anything a visitor types goes straight into the email and is never stored.

Never miss a check-in

If someone can't be emailed, LobbyApp alerts your office contact instead, so no one is left waiting.

Change it any time

Add or remove people from your dashboard. The iPad picks up changes within minutes, without touching it.

Walk-ins handled

Clients without an appointment see your office phone number so they can call to schedule.

Ready to try it?

Setting up takes about five minutes. All you need is an iPad.
